## 🚀 About Me

I'm a **Talent Development Executive** with a background in **Computer Science & Engineering**, passionate about connecting technology, learning, and career development.

I work with learners and job seekers to help them identify skill gaps, strengthen their technical profiles, improve their portfolios, prepare for interviews, and become more job-ready.

Alongside my professional work in talent development, I continue to explore **software development, web technologies, programming, and problem solving**.
